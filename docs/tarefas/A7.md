# A7 — Winter Veil in the village: a snowy map and three decorations

**Owner:** Codex (art only) · **Approves:** the owner · **Imports:** Claude · **Branch:** `codex/A7-winter-veil`
**Can start now.** **Deadline: 11/12** (Claude imports by 16/12, so the village is dressed while the festival runs in the
game, until 02/01). Context: `docs/masmorras.md` §13 ("Festas do calendário").

## What it is

While the game's **Feast of Winter Veil** runs, the village dresses for it: the map turns snowy and three decorations
appear on it. When the festival ends, everything goes back to the normal map. It is our own art in our own style: a cozy
winter festival, **not** a copy of the game's Winter Veil art (no Greatfather Winter, no Blizzard decorations).

## The style

The same hand as the approved high-resolution art in `arquivo/arte_v5/` (the map, the buildings, the cart, the
portraits): detailed pixel-art look, warm lantern light, wood and stone, the turquoise Korum colour, rich vegetation.
Compare every piece with `map@hd.png` and `cart@hd.png` before delivering.

## What to draw

### 1. The snowy map — `map_winter@hd.png`

- **1774 x 887**, exactly the size of `arquivo/arte_v5/map@hd.png`, and **drawn over it**: every road, lot, pond,
  rock, tree and lantern stays **exactly where it is**. Claude lays the two maps one over the other to check.
- Snow on the grass and on the tree tops, frozen edges on the ponds and the stream, the waterfall partly frozen, a few
  footprints on the roads. The **eight dirt lots stay clear** (the buildings stand on them) and so do the roads (lightly
  dusted is fine).
- Warmer lanterns (they glow more in the snow), maybe a few small strings of lights on the trees at the edges.
- `docs/tarefas/A7-mapa.jpg` shows the map with the eight building spots (red) and the cart (yellow): nothing may be
  painted over them.

### 2. Three decorations (standing on the map, like the buildings and the cart)

Each as `name@hd.png`, **512 x 512**, transparent background, the drawing **standing on the ground line y = 432**,
centred, with its soft shadow and room around it (the same rules as the buildings and `cart@hd.png`).

| File | What | Size of the drawing in the 512 | Where it goes (blue squares in `A7-mapa.jpg`) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `winter_tree@hd.png` | a tall pine decorated for the festival: turquoise and gold baubles, a garland of lights, a star on top, a few presents at its foot | about 300 wide, 400 tall | the grass between the Hall and the Workshop |
| `snowman@hd.png` | a friendly snowman with a turquoise Korum scarf, a lantern beside him | about 220 wide, 260 tall | the grass between the Market and the Inn |
| `gifts@hd.png` | a small pile of wrapped presents and a sled | about 260 wide, 180 tall | the grass north of the Hall, near the cart |

Claude places them on the map and sizes them with the buildings; you only draw them.
