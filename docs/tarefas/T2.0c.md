# T2.0c — The Market asks for Workshop items too (4 herb orders + 4 item orders), and the Workshop pays more

**Owner:** Claude (reassigned from Codex on 17/11: Codex now does only art and text review) · **Sprint:** F2.0 (economy) · **Estimate:** 5 h · **Branch:** `claude/T2.0c-market-goods`
 Read `docs/economia.md` (approved by the owner on 17/11) first: rules 3, 4 and 5 are this task.

## Why

Today the Market only asks for herbs (3 orders), and a Workshop item is often worth less than the herbs it used. The owner
wants the Market to ask for Workshop items too, with more choice, and **crafting must always give more coins and more xp
than the raw herbs it used**.

## What to do

### 1. Numbers (`Balance.lua`)

- `B.ORDERS`: replace `slots = 3` with `herbSlots = 4, goodsSlots = 4` (keep `slots` as their sum so old readers work).
  Add `goodsPayMultiplier = 1.6` and `goodsXpMultiplier` (pick it so rule 3 below holds; say the value in the PR).
- `B.CRAFT = {markup = 1.5, perMinute = ...}`: a Workshop item is worth `markup × (value of its inputs) + perMinute ×
  minutes of crafting`. The value of an input is its sell price: a herb's `sell`, `ore`/`fish` from `B.GOODS`.
- Each recipe in `B.RECIPES` gets an `xp` field (xp for **crafting** it, given when it finishes in `Workshop:Update()`).
- The `sell` of the crafted goods in `B.GOODS` (heal, mana, agil, stew, roast, feast, bar, tools, blade) is **no longer a
  fixed number**: `KH.Workshop.Value(recipeId)` computes it from `B.CRAFT`. Ore and fish keep their fixed `sell`.
  Everything that reads the sell price of a crafted good (Barn selling, `Village:GoodValue` or wherever it is) must use it.

### 2. Orders (`Farm.lua`, `Orders`)

- `db.orders` becomes 8 slots: **1–4 herb orders** (as today) and **5–8 item orders** `{good = recipeId, qty, coins, xp, npc}`.
- An item order only asks for a recipe the Workshop has **unlocked** (`Workshop:Unlocked`). With no Workshop (or no recipe
  open), slots 5–8 stay **empty** (`nil`): `Orders:Ensure()` fills them as soon as a recipe opens.
- Item order quantity: small (1–3 at first, a little more with the level; put the numbers in `B.ORDERS`).
  Pay: `qty × Workshop.Value(recipe) × goodsPayMultiplier`; xp: `qty × recipe.xp × goodsXpMultiplier`.
- `Orders:CanDeliver(slot)` / `Orders:Deliver(slot)` work for both kinds (an item order takes from `db.goods[recipe]`).
- Older saves have 3 orders: they stay as slots 1–3; `Ensure` fills 4–8. Nothing is lost (no migration needed: the
  defaults and `Ensure` cover it).

### 3. The rule the owner asked for — a test that holds it

`tests/test_economy_craft.py`: for **every** recipe, at the level where it opens:

- coins: `Workshop.Value(recipe)` > what the inputs sell for raw, **and** an item order for it pays more than herb orders for
  the same inputs would;
- xp: crafting xp + item-order xp > the xp of harvesting those herbs + delivering them in a herb order.

If a recipe fails, change the numbers (not the test).

### 4. Screens: only what is needed not to break

The Market screen will be redone by Claude (T2.0e: 8 orders in a new layout). In this task, change `UI/Market.lua` only so
it does not break with item orders and empty slots: an item row shows the item's icon (`GoodTex`) and name
(`T("good_" .. id)`), the Barn stock of that item, and an empty slot shows a short line ("Open the Workshop to get item
orders" — add the key in both languages). It is fine if 8 rows do not fit yet.

## Must not change

- The order of the day (`Daily.lua`).
- Herb numbers, levels, the XP curve, building costs: those are T2.0a/T2.0d (Claude), in parallel. Touch `B.PLANTS`,
  `B.HERBALISM`, `B.COST` **not at all**, so the two branches do not conflict.
