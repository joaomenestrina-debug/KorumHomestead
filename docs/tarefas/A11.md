## What it is

The homestead has **55 achievements** in **nine categories** (`Achievements.lua`; the screen opens from the trophy at the
top of the window). Today each uses an icon of the game. The owner wants **icons of our own, in the style of the
addon's art: one per category** (owner, 22/11: every achievement of the Garden shares the Garden's icon, and so on). The
game shows an icon **grey while the achievement is not done** and in its normal colours once it is: draw them in full
colour; the grey is made by the game (do not deliver grey copies).

Also: the portrait of the **Mayor** (`docs/prefeito.md`), who will speak the alerts of the village.

## The style

The art of the addon (`arquivo/arte_v5/*@hd.png`: the buildings, the map, the portraits): warm painted look, soft
light from one side, rich but simple colours, a little shine.

**Achievement icons:**
- square, **256 × 256**, with a thin **gold frame** of rounded corners (the gold of the followers' portraits) and a
  dark background inside;
- **one clear object** in the middle, readable at 36 px (that is the size on the screen); no text, no numbers;
- the nine side by side must read as one family (same frame, same light), each object different;
- the last one (`ach_final`) is the most special: the village logo inside a laurel, gems on the frame.

**The Mayor's portrait:** like the followers' (`follower1@hd.png`...: the same round gold frame, head and shoulders,
three-quarter view), **at least 256 × 256**, transparent corners. A human man in his sixties, neat grey moustache,
small round spectacles, a dark green coat with gold buttons and a mayor's chain of office, a quill behind his ear and a
thick ledger (the Book of Records) under his arm. Expression: a polite, slightly raised eyebrow, the face of someone who
has just noted something about you. Background: an office with shelves of papers and one sad potted plant.
