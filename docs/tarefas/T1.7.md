## Why

The owner tests in the game once a week. Pictures of every new screen, made by the simulator, let him and the reviewer
see the alpha before that, and are the screenshots of the CurseForge page later.

## What to do

In `ferramentas/simulador_ui.py`, after the `12-novidades` scenes, add (names exactly these, ptBR unless said):

| Picture | State |
|---|---|
| `13-fora-8h` | the `mid` homestead, last seen 8 hours ago (`db.lastSeen = now - 8 * 3600`), with plots ready, ore and fish made and one follower back: the "While you were away" card open |
| `13b-away-english` | the same in English |
| `14-mercado-encomenda-do-dia` | the Market with the order of the day not delivered, streak 2 |
| `14b-mercado-sequencia-7` | the Market right after delivering on day 7 of the streak (the prize shown) |
| `14c-mercado-dia-feito` | the Market after today's order was delivered (waiting for tomorrow) |
| `15-icone-encomenda-do-dia` | the icon on the screen with the dot of a waiting order of the day |

Build each state through the addon's own functions (`NS.Daily:Deliver()`, moving `NOW`/the clock), not by writing the
screen's fields, so a picture always shows what the game would show. Keep `newsSeen = 1000` in the states so the news
screen does not open over them.
