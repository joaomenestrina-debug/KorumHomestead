## What it is

From version 2.3 the village finds new followers in the dungeons, the raids and the calls of WoW Forever. Each one comes
from a place of the Forever and has a rarity (Rare, Epic, Legendary). They need portraits that sit beside the six of
today without anyone noticing a different hand.

## The style

Exactly like the six portraits of today (`arquivo/arte_v5/follower1@hd.png` … `follower6@hd.png`;
`docs/tarefas/A8-seguidores-atuais.png` shows them side by side):

- the **same round frame**: gold, ornate, with the small turquoise gems at the four points;
- head and shoulders, three-quarter view, a warm smile or a calm look, warm lantern light from one side;
- a background that tells where the follower is from (the six have a workshop, a forge, a garden...);
- the frame whole inside the image, with transparent corners.

**The rarity is not painted:** every frame is the same gold; the game adds the rarity colour around it.

## The twelve (approved by the owner on 19/11)

The races and places come from WoW Forever (`docs/historia-forever.md`, checked on its Wowhead,
https://www.wowhead.com/forever/pt); the names are ours and must not be names of characters of the game.

**The Skyborne look** (from the game's models): elves with **blue-lavender skin**, **turquoise hair**, long ears and
softly glowing eyes; clothes in cream and white with brown leather and turquoise details; white, grey and glowing blue
weapons. Draw them in our style, not a copy of the game's models.
