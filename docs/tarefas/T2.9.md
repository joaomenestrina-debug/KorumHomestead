**Owner:** Codex (art only) · **Approves:** the owner · **Imports:** Claude · **Sprint:** 2 · **Branch:** `codex/T2.9-event-art`
**Can start now.** Read `docs/eventos.md` (approved on 17/11): the events bring visitors to the village.

## The style

Copy the style of the approved art in `arquivo/arte_v3/` (and its `LEIA-ME.md`): detailed pixel art, warm lantern light,
wood and stone, the turquoise Korum banner, rich vegetation. Every new piece must look made by the same hand. Look at
`hall1@2x.png`, `market2@2x.png`, `inn2@2x.png` for buildings and `follower1@2x.png` … `follower6@2x.png` for portraits.

## What to draw

### 1. The visitors' cart (on the village map)

- Files: `cart.png` **128 x 128** (the real pixel grid) and `cart@2x.png` **256 x 256** (the same, doubled, no smoothing).
  Transparent background.
- A travelling merchant's cart: wooden wagon with a cloth canopy (turquoise stripes, like the Market), crates, sacks and
  barrels, a small lantern, a hanging sign. Optional: a pony or mule.
- Smaller than a building: the drawing about **70 px wide and 70 px tall on the 128 grid**, standing on the ground line
  **y = 108** (y = 216 on the @2x), centred, soft shadow included.
- It stands on the road in the middle of the map, between the two rows of buildings (it covers no building and no sign).

### 2. Three portraits (on the cards of the Visitors screen)

- Files: `visitor_merchant.png`, `visitor_caravan.png`, `visitor_messenger.png`, each **32 x 32** plus `@2x` **64 x 64**,
  framed exactly like the followers' portraits (round face crop, same light and outline).
- **Merchant:** well-dressed trader, feathered hat, gold coin pouch (the generous merchant who pays double).
- **Caravan master:** weathered traveller, scarf and hood, a map or a rope (the trading caravan).
- **Messenger:** young courier in a hurry, satchel, a sealed letter (the urgent order).
