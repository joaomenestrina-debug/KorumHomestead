## Why

The owner asked for characters who say the alerts and the news of the village, with humour. The player is the owner
of the village; five people run it for them, each commenting their own part: **Steward Ambrose** (the village as a
whole), **Foreman Dorota** (Garden, Mine, Pond, Barn, Workshop), **Clerk Tiberius** (the Market), **Butler Fenwick**
(the visitors) and **Advisor Marga** (the Inn and the followers). Who they are and how each one talks is in
**[`docs/personagens.md`](../personagens.md)**: read it first, the whole of it. The code is ready (`Voices.lua`): for
each moment below, the game picks one line at random and puts the name of who says it before it; a moment without
lines keeps the plain text of today.

## What to write

For every moment of the table, **in `Locales.lua`**, in `L.enUS` and in `L.ptBR`:

- the keys `say_<moment>_1`, `say_<moment>_2`, ... (for `readyAlert`: `say_readyAlert_1`, `say_readyAlert_2`...),
  in the voice of **who says that moment** (the column *Who* below);
- **5 lines** for the moments that happen many times a day (`readyAlert`, `headReadyOne`, `headReadyMany`,
  `craftDone`, `innBackOne`, `delivered`, `hello`), **3 lines** for the others, **2** for the rare ones (`capLotus`,
  `advRaid`, `streakRare`);
- the same number of lines in both languages (the game counts the English ones).

Put them together in one block near the end of each language, after the line `who_steward = ...`, one moment per line, grouped by character.

## The rules of each line

- It keeps **every code of the plain text, in the same order** (`%s`, `%d`). A literal percent sign is written `%%`.
  `tests/test_formatos.py` checks it.
- It says **the fact first** (what is ready, how much, who): a player who does not laugh must still understand it.
- **About 110 characters at most**: it shows beside the icon on the screen and in the chat.
- The voice of the character, as `personagens.md` describes it. **Nothing from Portal** (no cake, science, testing, chambers, neurotoxin, no known
  line). No cruelty, no swearing, no real-world topics; "T" rating.
- Brazilian Portuguese as a player talks; the Portuguese joke does not need to be the English one translated, but the
  fact is the same. No em dash (—).
- Lore of WoW Forever only from [`historia-forever.md`](../historia-forever.md).
