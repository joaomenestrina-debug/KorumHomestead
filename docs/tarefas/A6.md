## What to look at in every piece

1. **Cut:** nothing touches the edge of the image; buildings stand on the ground line **y = 108** (216 on the @2x),
   centred, inside the 128 x 128, with their soft shadow whole.
2. **Quality:** clean pixel art at the real grid (no blur, no smeared or half-transparent edges, no stray pixels, no
   noise); the same light (warm lanterns), outline and palette as the T2.9 art and the approved buildings.
3. **Growth:** level 1 > 2 > 3 of each building grows in the same place and keeps its identity.
4. **The @2x is exactly the real grid doubled** (nearest neighbour), never a different drawing.
5. **The map:** the eight building spots stay where they are (`arquivo/arte_v3/LEIA-ME.md` has the coordinates); only fix
   quality, never move the roads or the spots.
