# Changelog

## 2.1.0-alpha4
- The minimap button shows the village logo, the same as the icon on the screen.
- The minimap button counts and lists the same as the icon on the screen: visitors and the time they stay, plots ready, orders you can deliver and followers back.
- The path at the top of the window no longer runs into "Village" in English.

## 2.1.0-alpha2
The visitors stay a bonus at every level, and their texts were reviewed.

**Visitors**
- At high levels the urgent orders and the merchants ask for or buy fewer units of late herbs and Workshop items, so each visit stays a bonus next to the Garden; the price is still double.
- The rare Firefin Snapper of the Pond shows its own name and price (it was taking the ones of the Mud Snapper of the Workshop).
- The texts of the visitors were reviewed in both languages; "XP" is now written the same way everywhere.

## 2.1.0-alpha1
Visitors come to the village, and all its art was redrawn.

**Visitors**
- Now and then, while you play, someone comes: an urgent order, a generous merchant, a trading caravan, a rare fish in the Pond or a rare vein in the Mine.
- A cart by the Hall glows and hops while someone is there; a click opens the new Visitors screen, with what each one wants, how long they stay and the answer (deliver, sell, trade, collect).
- The alert beside the icon on the screen says who came and how long they stay. If the time runs out, the chance and its xp go with them; nothing you have is taken.
- "While you were away" tells which visitors left.

**Art**
- The map, every building at each level, the followers and the visitors were redrawn in high resolution, made to look sharp at the size they show.

**Other changes**
- A sparkle at the top of the window opens "What's new" whenever you want; the screen now scrolls (bar, drag or mouse wheel).
- The Barn shows the space of the herbs, the ore and the fish apart; the Mine and the Pond show their own stock, and the Hall recommends a bigger Barn when ore or fish are filling it.
- The description of the Mine and the Pond no longer gets cut.

## 2.0.0
The big update of the homestead. Your 1.0 village is carried over: nothing you have is lost.

**Village level and economy**
- Your Village now has its own level, from 1 to 60, in place of Herbalism. Your old level becomes the Village level that keeps every herb you had (Herbalism 20 becomes Village 37).
- 27 herbs, each opening at the Village level of its Herbalism skill in the game divided by 5, from Peacebloom to Icecap.
- Harvesting gives a little xp; Market orders and the Workshop give most of it. At level 60 the xp turns into Black Lotus keepsakes.
- Buildings ask for a Village level and their cost follows what the Garden earns, so the village lasts much longer.
- The Hall shows the best upgrade for now ("Recommended").

**Help to decide**
- The Hall marks the best upgrade for now ("Recommended").
- The Workshop marks what the Market is asking for (or the craft worth the most), and the Garden says which seed the Market needs.

**Market**
- 4 herb orders and 4 Workshop item orders.
- An order of the day that pays more, with a streak of days and prizes in xp and Black Lotus.

**Workshop**
- 21 recipes, one per trade at each Workshop level, with the game's items (from Minor Healing Potion to Arcanite Bar).
- Crafting always pays more coins and xp than the raw materials. The queue starts with 3 places.

**Garden**
- A click on an empty plot opens the seed menu: every herb at once, the amount of plots, or all of them.
- A growing plot can be pulled up with the × (the seed is lost).

**Coming back**
- "While you were away": what happened while the window was closed, with "Collect all".
- The icon on the screen says in words what waits, and a click opens the place.
- A "What's new" screen opens once after each update.
- The window has a zoom at the top, from 60% to 150%.
- Texts that ran out of the window on some screens now fit.

## 1.0.1
- Ore, fish, potions, meals and tools now use the icons of items of the game, like the herbs.
- The Barn no longer runs past the window with many kinds of goods; the Workshop queue shows how many wait beyond the last row.

## 1.0.0
- First public release.
- Village map with eight buildings: Hall, Garden, Barn, Market, Mine, Pond, Workshop and Inn.
- Workshop with three trades: Alchemy, Cooking and Smithing.
- Inn with six followers and missions.
- A "how this works" card the first time each place is opened, and an in-game guide.
- Floating icon with notifications; minimap button.
- English and Brazilian Portuguese.
