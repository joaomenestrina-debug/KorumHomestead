# Changelog

## 1.0.1
- Ore, fish, potions, meals and tools now use the icons of items of the game, like the herbs.
- The Barn no longer runs past the window with many kinds of goods; the Workshop queue shows how many wait beyond the last row.

## 1.0.0
- First public release.
- Village map with eight buildings: Hall, Garden, Barn, Market, Mine, Pond, Workshop and Inn.
- Workshop with three trades: Alchemy, Cooking and Smithing.
- Inn with six followers and missions.
- A "how this works" card the first time each place is opened, and an in-game guide.
- Floating icon with notifications; minimap button.
- English and Brazilian Portuguese.
