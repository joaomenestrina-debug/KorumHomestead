# Korum Homestead

A little idle village for the moments of waiting in World of Warcraft: the queue for a dungeon, the flight, the
boat, the minutes before the raid. Grow herbs, build up a village, craft in its workshop and send followers on
missions. Everything goes on by the clock, so the village keeps working while the game is closed.

Korum Homestead is a minigame of its own. It does not touch your character, your bags or your gold: its coins are its
own, and nothing in the game is given or taken. It needs no other addon.

## How to play

Open it with `/kh`, the icon on the screen or the minimap button. The window opens on the map of the village: every
building is a place to click. The first time you open a place, a short card tells how it works (the **?** beside its
title brings it back).

- **Garden** - pick a seed and click an empty plot. Each herb grows in its own time; click a ready plot to harvest.
  Every harvest raises the Herbalism of the homestead and opens better seeds.
- **Barn** - what you harvest or make waits here, to be sold.
- **Market** - orders from the people of the village, that pay more than selling and give xp.
- **Hall** - builds and improves every building, one work at a time.
- **Mine and Pond** - make ore and fish by themselves, every hour.
- **Workshop** - three trades in one queue: Alchemy (herbs into potions), Cooking (fish into meals) and Smithing
  (ore into bars and tools).
- **Inn** - shelters up to six followers. Send them on missions for coins and ore; they get better with every three.
- **Guide** - the **?** at the top: first steps, the buildings, the table of herbs and some tips.

The icon on the screen is the homestead minimized: drag it anywhere, click it to open the window. The number on it is
what waits for you, and the notifications come out of it. The window closes by itself when a fight starts.

## Details

- English and Brazilian Portuguese (chosen in the settings, or by the game's language).
- The names and icons of the herbs come from the game itself, in your language.
- One homestead per account: every character plays in the same village.
- Saved data: `KorumHomesteadDB`.
- Commands: `/kh` opens the window, `/kh harvest` harvests what is ready, `/kh help`.
